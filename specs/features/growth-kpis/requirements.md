# Growth and health KPIs: requirements

*Branch: `feat/growth-kpis` (on #96) · Roadmap item: #14 · Status: Agreed*

Owner (22 Nov): Control Center "best of best". The overview showed money and the pipeline, not how the business grows.

## Must
**Growth & Health** tab on the overview (platform admins), one call (`dashboard.growth`):
- KPIs: MRR (with % change vs 3 months ago), annual run rate, paying schools and revenue per school, new schools this
  month, schools lost in 30 days and churn %, collection rate (collected ÷ invoiced, 90 days), owed and overdue.
- Charts (12 months): MRR trend (invoices covering each month), cash in vs costs vs commission, new vs lost schools.
- What schools owe by age (not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, over 90 days); schools by county (new optional **County** on the
  school, 47 counties); paying schools and MRR by plan.
