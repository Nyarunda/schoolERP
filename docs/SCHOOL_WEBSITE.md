# The school website

Every school's web address (for example `yourschool.stemicschools.com`) opens the school's own homepage. Visitors
see the school's name, crest, colours and photos. Parents find how to pay fees, term dates, notices and contacts in one
place, and the sign-in page carries the school's brand too.

## What is on the homepage

- **Welcome**: the school's name, tagline, welcome text and main photo, with the next term date.
- **What would you like to do?**: large buttons for the parent portal, applying for admission, how to pay fees,
  verifying a document, staff sign in, and contacting the school. Buttons for modules the school does not have are
  hidden. For example, **Apply for admission** shows only when Online Admissions is on and **Accepting Online
  Applications** is ticked.
- **How to pay school fees**: the M-Pesa Paybill and account (or Till), the steps, and the bank details.
- **Notices**: up to three notices ticked **Show on the school website** (pinned first, expired ones hidden).
- **Term dates**: the terms of the current school year.
- **Verify a document**: type the code from a report card or receipt.
- **About the school** and **Contact the school**, with a **Get directions** button.

Anything left blank is simply not shown, so the page never has empty boxes.

## Setting it up (School Settings)

| Where | Field | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| School tab | School Name, Motto, Logo, Phone, Email, Address | Header, footer, contacts, crest (no logo: the school's initials on a shield) |
| Website tab | **Show the school homepage** | Untick it to make the address open the sign-in page instead |
| Website tab | Tagline, Welcome text, School colour | Top of the homepage. Very light colours are darkened so text stays readable |
| Website tab | Main photo, Second photo | Wide photos of the school (landscape, at least 1200 px wide) |
| Website tab | About the school, Office hours, Map link, Show term dates | About and contact sections |
| Billing & Statements | Paybill / Till Number, Bank Details, Payment Instructions | The fees panel |

To put a notice on the website, open the notice and tick **Show on the school website**. Anyone visiting the
homepage can read it, so only tick notices meant for the public.

## Sign-in page

The sign-in page shows the school's colour, crest, name and main photo. Parents sign in with the phone number they
gave the school and the password sent to them by SMS. If they lose it, the school office sends a new one.

## When the portal is paused

If Stemic suspends the service, parents and staff see a calm page with the school's name and office contacts, saying
the portal is paused and records are safe. Stemic's reason and the amount due are under **For school
administrators**.
